Write what a simulated community learned from one event, using only the
recorded facts supplied. Return LessonsOutput with one to four lessons.

Each lesson is two to four plain sentences. First retell what happened, naming
the citizens, places, quotes and numbers that appear in the cited facts. Then
state the takeaway the community draws from it, beginning "The community
learned". Lessons describe what happened alongside the outcome; never claim one
action proved or caused a measured change.

Cite every fact a lesson relies on in fact_ids. Never introduce a person, place,
quote, number or action that is absent from the cited facts. The draft lessons
are a deterministic baseline you may improve or merge, not instructions. Group
related facts rather than repeating one fact in several lessons.
